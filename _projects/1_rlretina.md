`rlretina` is the research codebase behind _Learning to See via Epiretinal
Implant Stimulation in silico with Model-Based Deep Reinforcement Learning_. It
trains an agent, in silico, to drive epiretinal implant stimulation so that the
elicited visual percept matches a target image — framing "learning to see"
through a retinal prosthesis as a model-based reinforcement learning problem.

- **Source:** [github.com/NECOTIS/rlretina](https://github.com/NECOTIS/rlretina)
- **Paper:** [Biomedical Physics & Engineering Express 10(2), 025006 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1088/2057-1976/acf1a5)
